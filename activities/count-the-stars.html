Count the Stars

Counting with meaning. The child counts a group of objects and matches it to a numeral, building one-to-one correspondence rather than rote chanting.

Skills practised

  • Early numeracy
  • One-to-one matching
  • Counting

Try it off screen

Once your child finishes, repeat the same idea with real objects around the house — that is where the learning settles.
